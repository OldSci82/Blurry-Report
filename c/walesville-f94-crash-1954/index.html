Walesville F-94 crash (Jul 2, 1954)

CaseWalesville, New York, near Utica · Posted Nov 27, 2026

An Air Force jet went after an unknown target over New York. Minutes later its crew bailed out and the jet fell on a small town.
Walesville, New York, near Utica. July 2, 1954.

According to records gathered by NICAP researchers, an F-94C Starfire was diverted from a training mission to an active air defense mission near the Griffiss Air Force Base patrol zone. A Pentagon spokesman told reporters the unidentified plane was about 75 miles northeast of Rome when the jets intercepted it. As the jet closed in, the story goes, a sudden, unbearable heat filled the cockpit. The pilot and the radar officer ejected and survived.

The empty jet came down in Walesville, and four people on the ground were killed. Newspapers at the time reported a blaze in the cockpit, and Project Blue Book's own index lists a UFO sighting at Walesville that day, with no evaluation given.

The debate: Major Donald Keyhoe made it famous as a UFO chase gone wrong. Researcher Kevin Randle argued it was an ordinary cockpit fire with no UFO involved. NICAP's Fran Ridge says it is not just a jet crash with a UFO added later.
