Titusville FL slim black upright figure in Merritt Island NWR marsh, 8 mi north of Kennedy Space Center (summer 2023)

CaseTitusville, Florida · Posted Nov 11, 2026

Eight miles from the Kennedy Space Center launch pads, a 77 year old woman started yelling at her husband to turn the car around.
Titusville, Florida. Merritt Island National Wildlife Refuge, Black Point Wildlife Drive at Max Brewer Parkway, summer 2023, midday.

Gail and Bob Kent, retirees from Cocoa who grew up in Ohio, were taking a slow drive through the Black Point Wildlife Drive area of the refuge. The weather was clear and dry. As Bob made the left turn onto Max Brewer Parkway, Gail looked out across an open field of waist high grass toward a stand of tall trees.

Something was walking through it, less than 100 yards away. Upright on two legs, covered in black fur, slimmer than she ever expected a bigfoot to be, slightly hunched and moving fast, with long arms swinging. She watched it for maybe 10 to 15 seconds as the car turned, then started yelling, "Stop! Stop! That's a bigfoot! Turn around!" By the time Bob had the car facing the field, it had walked into the trees and was gone. Bob never saw it.

BFRO founder Matthew Moneymaker interviewed both of them by phone and calls them very credible. The refuge covers 140,000 acres with deer, wild boar and, officially, black bears that are rarely seen. Gail is adamant it was not a bear, which cannot walk quickly upright with long swinging arms. Moneymaker also notes that one of the best reports the BFRO has ever received came from the same refuge in 2011, about 20 miles north, when a fishing guide and two clients had a close daylight encounter in the mangroves of Mosquito Lagoon.

Anything living out there has heard a lot of rocket launches.

Could something really be hiding in the marsh next to the space center?
