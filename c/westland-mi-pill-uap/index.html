Westland pill-shaped UAP

CaseWestland, Michigan · Posted Oct 28, 2026

Westland, Michigan. October 13, 2026, about 9:10 a.m.

Under a clear sky, near Wayne Road and Warren Road, a witness watched a "large, large white pill shaped object with a dark circle in the center quietly descending at 45 degrees." It was down at treetop level and made no sound, and the sighting lasted somewhere between one and five minutes. "Thought at first glance that the object was a large drone," they wrote in their report to UFOIndex, "but cannot confirm that there is a drone that is manufactured with this description."

That might have been the end of it. Then the comments started. On October 17, a reader wrote, "We just saw the same cigar object in Grosse Pointe today," at 1:55 p.m. And on October 22, another said they'd seen "something identical in central Iowa two days ago. Pill shape, blurry black center. Low and silent. Moved too fast to be a balloon and too slow to be a plane," gliding east to west at a constant altitude for about four minutes.

Three sightings, three places, one oddly specific shape.
