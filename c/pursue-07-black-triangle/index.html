PURSUE Release 07 black triangle

CaseWestern United States · Posted Oct 27, 2026

Western United States. June 2026, around dusk.

The Pentagon's seventh batch of UFO files, PURSUE Release 07, went up at war.gov/UFO on October 23, and its lead case is only four months old. Federal special agents at a national security site in the western U.S. say they watched a matte black triangle rise silently over a ridge, hover, and then dive into a canyon.

AARO's memo describes a "matte black triangular shape of extremely low aspect ratio" with a 13 to 15 foot wingspan: a slow climb of about 10 seconds, a hover of about 20, then a two to three second "terrain-conforming descent into the canyon." No sound, no exhaust, no seams, and a "spatially uniform" heat signature. A ground survey the next morning "located no wreckage, ground scarring, or vegetation damage." The government's index also mentions a second sighting the following evening, which wasn't released.

Here's the catch. The FBI's record of the same agents' signed statement says "The total duration of the sighting was approximately three seconds," mentions no hover, and calls the object "most resembling a delta wing drone." The images in the FBI file are a black shape drawn onto a daytime photo of the terrain, not photos of the object. And the "1,800 mph" figure going around isn't in either document.

Trained agents, thermal gear, and two accounts of one evening that don't match. Still unresolved.
