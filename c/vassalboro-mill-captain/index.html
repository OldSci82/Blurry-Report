Vassalboro woolen mill ghost "The Captain"

CaseVassalboro, Maine · Posted Oct 30, 2026

Vassalboro, Maine. The old American Woolen Company mill.

Local man Ray Breton bought the mill property for $35,500 in 2010. He says the original wooden building burned in the late 1700s, and the brick wings that replaced it are "dated almost for every war there was," from an 1850 wing to one built in 1917. At its peak, he says, the mill employed about 500 people.

He also says the accident log is long: 33 drownings over 100 years, electrocutions, and someone allegedly pushed down the elevator shaft. And one spirit has made himself especially noticeable. They call him "The Captain," an old man in what looks like a captain's hat, seen in the backs of photographs and in first-hand accounts.

This October, Central Maine Ghost Hunters took their electromagnetic sensors, lights, radios and bells inside to try to reach him. They say they are more in the business of helping ghosts than hunting them.
