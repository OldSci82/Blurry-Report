Bregant House doll house EVP investigation

CaseCouncil Bluffs, Iowa · Posted Oct 28, 2026

Council Bluffs, Iowa. The Bregant House, October 2026.

People in Council Bluffs call it the doll house. It was built in 1912 for Jean and Inez Breck, two vaudeville performers who met at Coney Island. According to Corrine Peterson of the Pottawattamie County Historical Society, Inez was about three and a half feet tall and Jean about three and three quarters. Jean died in the 1940s and Inez in 1969.

This month, Iowa Western Community College's new continuing education course, "Haunted Bluffs," took its students through the city's spookiest spots: the Squirrel Cage Jail, the Rail Museum, a cemetery, and the Bregant House, where they ran what was described as the first paranormal investigation ever held inside. "I don't think anybody has ever been in here long enough to maybe even try to experience anything," said instructor Josh Baker.

The class brought "the box," temperature meters, voice recorders and EMF meters. A week later they sat down to go through the evidence and found five EVPs. On one track, Baker says, "It's either a very low kind of sigh, that was real weird."

One student said she didn't feel much inside. The recorder may disagree.
