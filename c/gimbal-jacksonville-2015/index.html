Navy GIMBAL video off Jacksonville (Jan 2015)

CaseOff Jacksonville, Florida · Posted Nov 27, 2026

"There's a whole fleet of them." A Navy pilot said it on tape while his jet's camera locked onto something rotating over the Atlantic.
Off Jacksonville, Florida. F/A-18 Super Hornets from the USS Theodore Roosevelt, January 2015.

During training off the East Coast, Navy pilots flying from the Roosevelt kept picking up unknown objects on their instruments. One crew's infrared targeting camera recorded a dark oval shape with a glow around it. On the tape you can hear them: "Look at that thing," and "It's rotating," as the object holds against the wind and slowly tips on its side.

Lieutenant Ryan Graves, who flew with that strike group, later told 60 Minutes they were seeing these objects every day for at least a couple of years. The video, nicknamed GIMBAL, was published by The New York Times in December 2017. In April 2020 the Pentagon officially released it and said the objects were still unidentified.

The debate: skeptics such as Mick West argue the "rotation" is the camera's own gimbal turning and the glow is glare from a distant jet engine. Pilots and some analysts say that does not explain the radar returns or the fleet the crew described.
