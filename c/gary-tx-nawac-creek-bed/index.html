Gary TX NAWAC creek-bed hog hunters; bipedal something in dry creek (Oct 17, 2026)

CaseGary, Texas · Posted Nov 13, 2026

Gary, Texas. A dry creek bed on a Panola County deer lease, October 17, 2026, about 8 PM.
Two hog hunters dropped a carcass into a pitch black creek bed, and something down there took off running on two legs.

A cold snap had blown through that day. The son, 21, and his father, 52, had shot two hogs too big to eat, so they drove them to the dry creek bed where they have left deer, hog and coyote remains for years. It sits just off a dirt and sand road, six to eight feet straight down, with dense brush along the edge and pine and oak beyond. At night it is so dark down there that, as the 6 foot 2 son puts it, he could stand up straight where the carcasses land and nobody on the road would see him.

The first hog landed on a branch and knocked some rocks loose. Almost immediately something took off running on two legs for about three seconds, then stopped dead. Both men pulled pistols and flashlights. They saw nothing. Then whatever it was started "freaking out," crushing tree limbs and throwing up dead leaves, but it never ran off. It stayed just out of sight. They dropped the second hog fast and left. The son has not been back.

Earlier, just before dark, he had heard a hog screaming in the woods that connect to that creek, for three or four minutes, with something crashing into trees. Then it stopped, and there was nothing, not even birds or bugs. He says the lease has always felt like he was being watched. He has found wrist thick branches snapped off, and one night from his deer stand he heard howls. NAWAC investigator Phil Burrows, who interviewed him, calls the report credible and the witness truthful.

Would you have climbed down into that creek bed with a flashlight?
