Boxford crawling figure (Topsfield Fair)

CaseBoxford, Massachusetts · Posted Oct 29, 2026

Boxford, Massachusetts. October 12, 2026, at night.

A high school senior was driving friends home from the Topsfield Fair, one of the oldest country fairs in America, on the back roads through Boxford. As the headlights crossed a dark lawn, she saw a figure on all fours. It had a human-like shape, but it was down low on the grass like an animal.

What stuck with her was how it moved. As the car came closer, the figure did not turn and run. It crawled backward, without ever turning around, and slipped into the shadows at the edge of the yard.

When the story got around, the first reaction from more than one person was "Oh, the Dogman?" There is no photo, no exact address and no police report, and nobody else has come forward. Just a teenager and her friends, a fair night in Essex County, and something that backed away from the light.
