Newcomerstown OH window face

CaseNewcomerstown, Ohio · Posted Nov 17, 2026

Three times in one week, a face more than 8 feet off the ground looked in through the same living room window.
Newcomerstown, Ohio. A house on a hill in Tuscarawas County, the first week of November 2026.

It had been an unusually hot week, so the window was open. The homeowner was on the couch, looked up and saw a face looking back. "The moment it realized I had noticed it," they wrote, "it ducked down and disappeared from view." They shut the window and pulled the shade.

A couple of days later, half convinced they had imagined it, they opened it again. Reading alone after dinner, they heard the dog start whining. "I looked up, and there it was again, right outside the window." This time its head was tilted slightly, "almost as though it were curious," and again it moved incredibly fast out of sight. The next night they waited for hours with a phone ready. Nothing. Then at about 3:30 AM, up for a drink, they saw it a third time through the closed glass. "But I saw it. Very clearly."

They describe a wide, elongated face with dark tan skin, large soft looking eyes, ears flat against the head, broad shoulders and hair. Not angry, just curious. They put it at over 8 feet after their 6 foot son-in-law stood outside the same window. They also say a son saw a large silhouette run from the woods about two weeks earlier.

The debate: there is no photo or video, and the house sits on a slope, so the window may be higher off the ground outside than it looks from the couch, which makes any height estimate shaky. Some readers said to treat it like a prowler and call the police. Cameras now watch the window, and so far it has not returned.

Would you ever open that window again?
