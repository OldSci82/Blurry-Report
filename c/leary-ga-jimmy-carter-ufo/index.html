Leary GA Jimmy Carter signed UFO report (1969; forms at Carter Presidential Library)

CaseLeary, Georgia · Posted Nov 12, 2026

A future president of the United States signed a UFO report, and it is still on file at his presidential library.
Leary, Georgia. Outside the Leary Lions Club, shortly after dark, about 7:15 PM, 1969.

Jimmy Carter was a Georgia state senator and a Lions Club district governor. He was standing outside with about ten club members, waiting for a 7:30 meeting where he was due to speak, when someone pointed west. About 30 degrees above the horizon, past a stand of pines, was a bright light, at times as big as the moon.

In his own words, it "seemed to move toward us from a distance, stopped, moved partially away, returned, then departed. Bluish at first, then reddish, luminous, not solid." No sound. He guessed it was 300 to 1,000 yards away. After 10 to 12 minutes it moved off into the distance and disappeared.

In September 1973, as Governor of Georgia, he filled out sighting forms for the International UFO Bureau and NICAP, listed ten fellow Lions as witnesses, and gave permission to use his name. Both forms are held at the Jimmy Carter Presidential Library.

The debate: his form says October 1969, but Lions Club records put the Leary visit on January 6, 1969. Skeptic Robert Sheaffer says it was Venus, which hung bright in the west that night. A former Air Force and NASA rocket scientist argued it was a glowing barium cloud released by a rocket from Eglin Air Force Base about half an hour earlier. Carter, who owned a telescope, said he knew Venus when he saw it. He also never claimed it was alien.

Venus, a rocket experiment, or something a future president could not explain?
