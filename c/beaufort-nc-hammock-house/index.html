The Stain on the Stairs

HauntBeaufort, NC · Posted Nov 25, 2026

They say a bloodstain on the stairs of this old house will not scrub out, and the young officer who died on those steps was buried standing up, facing England.
Beaufort, North Carolina. The Hammock House, on a low rise near Taylor Creek.

The Hammock House is named for the old word for a low hill, a rise so noticeable that ships coming in from the sea once used it as a landmark. The story goes that it was an inn in Beaufort's busy colonial port days, and that a wealthy captain with a fierce temper, known as "Mad" Brothers, sailed home the night of a farewell dance there. He burst into the ballroom, saw his bride to be dancing in the arms of a British naval lieutenant, and drew his sword. He chased the man up the stairs and stabbed him through the heart. Only then did he learn the lieutenant was her brother. Visitors still describe a dark stain on the steps, and the legend points to an upright grave in Beaufort's Old Burying Ground. Locals add that Blackbeard himself stayed at the inn.

The debate: some local historians date the house to around 1800, decades after Blackbeard died in 1718, and no record has ever confirmed the duel or the names in the story. Believers say the stairs speak for themselves.

Would you walk up those stairs at night?
