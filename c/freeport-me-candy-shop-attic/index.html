What the Actual Fudge candy shop attic haunting (GHOST team)

CaseFreeport, Maine · Posted Nov 2, 2026

Freeport, Maine. 7 Mill Street.

Kathy Welle was working in her candy shop, What the Actual Fudge, when she heard her husband go into a locked room upstairs, set down something heavy, and walk around. She started talking to him as she climbed the stairs. When she opened the door, nobody was there. It was only the latest in months of strange things inside the historic building.

An employee called in the Ghost Hunting and Occult Study Team. On September 18, seven investigators stayed from 6:30 PM to 5:30 AM with the lights off, recording all three floors. The focus kept coming back to the attic. Their rem pod lit up there a couple of times, and lead investigator Mike Greenleaf said, "It felt like something didn't want you there."

The recordings, they say, picked up loud banging, whimpering in the attic, references to a fire and violence, and a male voice yelling "get out."
