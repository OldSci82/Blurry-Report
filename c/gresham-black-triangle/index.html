Gresham black triangle UFO

CaseGresham, Oregon · Posted Oct 23, 2026

Gresham, Oregon. October 2, 2026, around 9 p.m.

Three people were sitting in their car at an intersection in Gresham, just after dark, when they spotted something low over a tree. According to the report they filed with the National UFO Reporting Center, it was a black triangle with lights in its corners, flying about 30 feet above the ground. The witness described it as a "dudley dorito" like object, and estimated it at 15 to 20 feet across, with the closest distance about 150 feet.

So they turned around and drove right to it. By the time they got there, the object was already near the horizon, heading north. And the tree it had been hanging over had branches ripped off of it.

The whole sighting lasted about 40 seconds, and the witnesses weren't sure of the exact time, only that it was after dark. NUFORC posted the report on October 6 as report 201344. Its October index also lists a second Gresham report from the night before, October 1, that reads "We were driving back from the store and saw like this dorito thing but it was like a boomerang."

There is no photo and the report is short. But three witnesses and a tree with fresh damage are not something you forget on the drive home.
