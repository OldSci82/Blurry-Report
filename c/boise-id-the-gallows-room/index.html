The Gallows Room

HauntBoise, ID · Posted Nov 26, 2026

Only one man was ever hanged in this prison's gallows room, and visitors say something in that cell house never left.
Boise, Idaho. The Old Idaho Penitentiary, in the foothills east of downtown.

The Old Pen held prisoners from 1872 to 1973, more than 13,000 of them, including Harry Orchard, who assassinated former Governor Frank Steunenberg, and Lyda Southard, the woman newspapers called Idaho's Lady Bluebeard. Riots over living conditions broke out in 1952, 1971 and 1973, and after the last one burned several buildings, the prison closed that December.

In 1957 Raymond Snowden was hanged in the gallows room of the maximum security cell house, the only execution ever carried out there. Visitors and paranormal teams, including Ghost Adventures, describe voices, being physically touched, and a heavy, strange feeling, most of all in the gallows room and in the solitary confinement cells known as Siberia. Ghost lore also talks about cell doors moving on their own and gasping sounds in the dark.

The debate: the Idaho State Historical Society, which runs the site, says there is no conclusive evidence of ghosts and leaves it to each visitor to decide. It also points out that only Snowden died in the gallows room, even though many retellings claim more hangings there. Its night investigations still sell out.

Would you spend a night in Siberia?
