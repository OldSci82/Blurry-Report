Two tall gray figures on US 95

CaseFallon-Lovelock, Nevada · Posted Oct 29, 2026

US 95 between Fallon and Lovelock, Nevada. October 10, 2026, about 10:30 p.m.

This stretch of highway is about as empty as it gets: sagebrush, alkali flats and dark mountains. A woman was driving it alone with a semi roughly 200 feet behind her when, she wrote, "I suddenly had a weird 'dread' feeling. Something I've never experienced."

Then she heard the semi hit its brakes hard. She figured a deer. But when she looked back at the road, two "human figures" sprinted across it ahead of her. "They were human form, but really tall and gray," she wrote. She saw legs, arms, even fingers, but no features, and "there was almost like a mist around them." In profile they looked completely smooth.

Afterward the truck backed way off and never tried to catch up, even when they were both 15 under the limit. "Did he see something?" Her husband, five to ten minutes behind her, saw nothing. Readers have pointed out the order of events doesn't quite add up, and there is no photo and no word from the trucker. She says the dread hasn't left.
