Dogman near Rocky Arbor State Park (yellow eyes)

CaseWisconsin Dells, Wisconsin · Posted Oct 20, 2026

Near Rocky Arbor State Park, Wisconsin Dells, Wisconsin. Reported October 5, 2026.

A driver was heading toward Wisconsin Dells to pick up an Applebee's order, top down on what they believe was a late-90s Chrysler Sebring convertible. It was the end of sunset, and they figured it would be a pretty ride. By the time they reached the stretch of highway that runs past Rocky Arbor State Park, a small park just outside the resort town, it was darker than they expected.

On the side of the road that isn't open to the public, there's a dirt road entrance where the driver says they've seen police park from time to time. That's where something was standing. "But standing there clear as day was a large creature 9-12 feet I'm guessing," they wrote on Reddit, "with legs that looked like the back legs of a dog but a huge chest and arms like a human and a dogs face with bright yellow eyes."

They say they made eye contact with it and knew it knew they had seen it. It didn't move. They floored it and called their mom right away. They've lived near there their whole life, had never heard of anything like it in that area, and say they sometimes still feel like it knows who they are.

Readers on the thread had little doubt about the label: dogman. Wisconsin is dogman country, home of the Beast of Bray Road near Elkhorn. But the driver came back with a detail that doesn't fit the usual reports. Everything they'd read said dogmen are only a little taller than a man. "This thing was way taller and wider."

There's no photo, no exact date for the sighting, and no report to police or the park. One reader suggested simply a bear. Another joked it was either a dogman or another annoying tourist from Illinois.
