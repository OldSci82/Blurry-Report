Hoh River Bigfoot across the river (BFRO 82613)

CaseHoh River near Forks, Washington · Posted Nov 2, 2026

Hoh River, near Forks, Washington. Around October 20, 1974, about noon.

It was opening day of deer season. Two friends had hunted the lower Hoh off Oil City Road that morning, then decided to drive into Olympic National Park to look for big bull elk. About halfway out on the park road, the passenger got very excited and told the driver to stop.

Across the Hoh River, standing in front of a big log, was a Bigfoot. The friend said it was running on two legs. It was sunny and clear. Neither of them had believed in Bigfoot until that day. "We know what we saw."

About 20 years later, the witness shared the story with a coworker who had fished the Hoh for years. Five years after that, the coworker stopped him in a hallway at work. His fishing buddy, a man he called very honest, had been driving into the Hoh one morning when a Bigfoot crossed the road twenty yards in front of him.
