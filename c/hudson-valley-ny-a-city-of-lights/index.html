A City of Lights

CaseHudson Valley, NY · Posted Nov 20, 2026

A police officer radioed in that a silent boomerang of lights as big as a football field was hovering right over her patrol car.
New Castle, New York, in the Hudson Valley. Cross Ridge Road near the Taconic State Parkway, about 9 PM, March 24, 1983.

Officer Andi Sadoff was parked on patrol when another officer came over the radio asking for backup because he was watching something incredible. She grabbed the mic: "Unit 5 to base, I see it too, it's hovering above my car. I'm following it." For about eight minutes she trailed a huge V of white and green lights until it slipped behind the hills. That night, police from Carmel to Yorktown were swamped with calls, a UFO hotline logged more than 300 reports, and drivers stopped on the Taconic to watch something they called a floating city glide over the trees at walking speed.

It was not a one night thing. People across Westchester, Putnam and Dutchess counties reported the same silent boomerang for years, and many of the witnesses were police officers on patrol.

The debate: state police traced the lights to a group of pilots flying small planes out of Stormville Airport in tight formation, with dark painted undersides and bright lights they could switch off all at once. The pilots called themselves the Martians. Sadoff and many others said they saw one solid object hovering in total silence, which small planes cannot do.

Could a few small planes fool a cop parked right underneath them?
