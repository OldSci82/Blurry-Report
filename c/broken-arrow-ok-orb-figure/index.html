Glowing orb and black figure (Nalusa Falaya?)

CaseBroken Arrow, Oklahoma · Posted Oct 19, 2026

Broken Arrow, Oklahoma. Around 5 a.m. on Sunday, October 4, 2026.

A night-shift worker in this Tulsa suburb steps out behind their apartment building with their boyfriend to take the dog out. It is still dark, with the first light just starting to show. Across a pond, past the parking lot, there is another apartment building, and under one of its outdoor lights something is glowing.

From a quarter to half a football field away, it looks like a glowing orb underneath or around the light. The boyfriend thinks it is probably a moth. "To me it looked like an actual glowing orb," the witness wrote. It stays in roughly the same area, moving slowly back and forth, disappearing and coming back. They decide to walk over for a closer look. It takes two or three minutes to get there, and when they arrive, there is nothing.

They head back to where they first stood. The boyfriend starts walking away, but the witness keeps staring at that spot. "As soon as he walked away I saw a completely black figure emerge from behind the building and what appeared to turn in my direction and just stood there." For five to ten seconds it stays there, a black human-shaped silhouette with no features they could make out. Then it moves smoothly back behind the building. The witness calls out, but by the time the boyfriend turns around, it is gone. The witness says they ended up crying.

They posted the account to Reddit's r/Humanoidencounters about three hours later, adding, "I'm not claiming this was paranormal." One commenter suggested the Nalusa Falaya, the "long black being" of Choctaw lore, a shadowy figure said to haunt the woods at dusk, and said it is also associated with floating lights. Dead of Night Dispatch, which wrote up the account, lays out the ordinary explanations too: a moth in the lamplight and an early-rising neighbor in dark clothing seen from a distance before dawn. There are no photos, and only one person saw the figure.
