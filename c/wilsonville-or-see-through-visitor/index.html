Wilsonville OR family home see-through silver object; 12-year-old's room flashed, NUFORC investigator Keith Bickford (August 25, 2026)

CaseWilsonville, Oregon · Posted Nov 16, 2026

Wilsonville, Oregon. A family home south of Portland, August 25, 2026, about 8 PM and again at 9:14 PM.
A 12 year old ran downstairs saying her whole room was flashing with light, and outside hung something silver and see-through, as wide as three airplanes.

The mother, identified only as S, was on the back patio a little after 8 when she saw something fall out of the sky, "almost like a bird was shot dead in the air and plummeted." In a blink it went from a rectangle to a large ball to a small ball, trailing a stream that looked like smoke, "but not smoke." Her partner and daughter only caught the trail.

At exactly 9:14 her daughter came running. The family hurried upstairs and saw "this big object floating still pointed directly towards our home." The girl described it as silver, with a distorted, see-through surface, two really big lights and no real shape, its nose tilted up. It hovered for almost a minute and then simply disappeared. She got one photo before it did.

Afterward the mother felt nauseous and off balance, and the girl had headaches on and off all night. Both were fine the next day. NUFORC investigator Keith Bickford interviewed the family and checked with two local farmers who fly crop drones day and night (none close to that size), the Clackamas County Sheriff's Office and Wilsonville Police (no reports that evening) and FlightRadar24 (nothing). He classified it as an anomalous sighting. The story spread this month after national outlets picked it up. Skeptics note the family's size and distance figures are nighttime estimates, and the photo has not been independently analyzed.
