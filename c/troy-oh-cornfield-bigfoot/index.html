Troy cornfield Bigfoot mocks the dog (BFRO 79110)

CaseTroy, Ohio · Posted Nov 2, 2026

Troy, Ohio. September 1, 2025, about 11:40 PM.

A young woman was taking the family German Shepherd out before bed when the dog alerted toward the back of the property, where the yard meets a cornfield and a set of train tracks. His hair stood straight up and he lunged so hard he pulled her forward.

Under the overhanging trees, she saw a greyish silver figure crouched low, making grunting sounds. As the dog stalked closer, it stood up, as tall as the corn, and began imitating the dog's bark as it moved off through the field. By the time her father and brother ran out to help, it had disappeared into the corn.

The family says it stayed motionless and small until the dog would not back off, and then it seemed to mock him. Afterward, the parent remembered scolding the kids for breaking down corn stalks. They all said it was not them.
