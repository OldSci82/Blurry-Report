Piney Woods TX Bigfoot photo holding deer

CasePiney Woods, Southeast Texas · Posted Oct 27, 2026

Piney Woods, Southeast Texas. Account posted October 15, 2026.

This Southeast Texas family goes looking for Bigfoot together, at night, in a national forest close to home, with audio recorders. According to the account the boy's parent sent to the Sasquatch Chronicles blog, the oldest son, Kayson, was walking down a trail with his grandmother to set up a recorder when "He felt a strange sensation on the back of his neck."

"He felt a mix of fear and adrenaline." He turned around and snapped a picture of the woods, "capturing a massive, dark figure standing between the trees." Then he ran, "leaving my poor mom behind," the parent wrote.

Looking at the photo closely, the family says "it appears that something is holding a deer." They enhanced the image and made a negative of it, and say you can see "the cheek, the nose, bridge, and one of the eyes" on the creature.

The account doesn't name the forest. Southeast Texas has four national forests, the Sam Houston, Davy Crockett, Angelina and Sabine, all in the Piney Woods. Dead of Night Dispatch, which wrote it up on October 25, points out the family was out searching for Bigfoot, and a single night photo, brightened and inverted, can show almost anything. But that kid knows what he felt on the back of his neck.
