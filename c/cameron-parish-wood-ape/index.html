Cameron Parish wood ape (NAWAC 25111401)

CaseCameron Parish, Louisiana · Posted Oct 28, 2026

Cameron Parish, Louisiana. November 2025, late afternoon, near the Sabine National Wildlife Refuge.

Two tugboat operators had docked their boats and gone for a walk along a road to stretch their legs. It was mostly cloudy with a light breeze off the Gulf, and the road ran through marsh grass two to four feet tall and small brushy trees. Then they heard rustling in the grass and brush, and about 80 to 100 yards out they saw a large brown animal moving through it, upright, with its head and shoulders two to four feet above the top of the grass.

One of them told North American Wood Ape Conservancy investigator Michael Mayfield his first thought was a bear walking on its hind legs. But this one was brown, not black, and there was a subtle grunting and wailing. It covered about 30 feet in 20 to 40 seconds before it either slipped behind taller brush or dropped down on all fours.

He's honest that he can't be sure the grunt didn't come from an unseen hog. But when his friend called a wildlife official to ask about bears, he was told there hadn't been a bear sighting in that area in more than twenty years.
