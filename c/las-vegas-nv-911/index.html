100% Not Human

CaseLas Vegas, Nevada · Posted Nov 19, 2026

A man called 911 and told the dispatcher two beings, 8 to 10 feet tall with big shiny eyes, were standing in his backyard looking at him.
Las Vegas, Nevada. A home in the northwest valley, just after midnight on May 1, 2023.

At about 11:50 PM on April 30, a Las Vegas Metro officer's body camera recorded a bright light streaking low across the sky. People from eastern California to Utah reported the same fireball. Roughly 40 minutes later, a man called 911. He said he, his father and his brother were working on a truck in the yard when something bright fell, they felt a big impact and heard footsteps. "I swear to God this is not a joke," he told the dispatcher. "They're 100% not human."

Officers arrived about half an hour later. "I have butterflies, bro," one said on the drive over. A family member told him it was a big creature, more than 10 feet tall, and the officer admitted another cop had seen something fall out of the sky too. When he walked into the backyard, police blacked out the video, citing privacy laws. Drone video later showed a circular imprint in the dirt.

The debate: NASA's planetary defense officer said the fireball was a small meteor, high up and hundreds of miles away, so nothing from it landed in anyone's yard. Skeptics point out that only two brothers said they saw the figures. Police closed the case. But a false 911 report is a felony in Nevada, and the family has stood by its story.

If you saw what they saw, would you have called 911?
