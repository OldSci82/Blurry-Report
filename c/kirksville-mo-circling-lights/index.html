Kirksville MO circling pulsing lights (Oct 19 and 25, NUFORC)

CaseKirksville, Missouri · Posted Nov 10, 2026

Kirksville, Missouri. October 19 and October 25, 2026.

A witness in Kirksville filed a report with the National UFO Reporting Center at the end of October, with video. In their own words: "Seen first on 10-19-2026 hovering high in the sky, then again multiple of them on the 25th after 9pm lower than before above the horizon circling around and slowly dimming and brightening in semi-sync and appear to be at least 2, maybe more objects."

So the first night it was a single light up high. Six nights later there were several, lower, circling each other and pulsing together.

KHMO's Doc Holliday says his first thought with any lights report now is drones, but the movement in the witness video does not look like drones to him. He also notes Kirksville is not near any military base, so it is unlikely to be aircraft out of Whiteman or Scott.

The video is posted with the report on NUFORC. Watch it and decide for yourself.
